\subsection{Classifier: Backbone, Test-Time Augmentation, and Calibration}

The diagnostic classifier is a ResNet-50~\citep{he2016resnet} with an
ImageNet-pretrained backbone~\citep{russakovsky2015imagenet} and a
seven-class linear head. Training uses standard ImageNet normalization,
moderate geometric and photometric augmentation, and a 256-pixel
evaluation crop. At inference, the classifier averages the softmax
probabilities of the original center-cropped image and its horizontal
flip. Let $z(x)\in\mathbb{R}^{7}$ be the logit vector for image $x$, let
$T>0$ be the temperature learned by post-hoc calibration on the internal
validation split, and let $\mathrm{flip}(x)$ denote the horizontal flip
of $x$. The deployed class distribution is
\begin{equation}
\bar{p}(c \mid x)
= \frac{1}{2}\left[
\mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\tfrac{z(x)}{T}\right)_c
+
\mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\tfrac{z(\mathrm{flip}(x))}{T}\right)_c
\right],
\label{eq:tta-calibration}
\end{equation}
and the predicted label is
\begin{equation}
\hat{y}(x) = \arg\max_{c \in \mathcal{C}} \bar{p}(c \mid x),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{C}$ is the seven-class label set. The current deployed
checkpoint uses a learned temperature $T = 0.7513$ obtained by minimizing
negative log-likelihood on the held-out validation split, following the
single-parameter temperature-scaling recipe of
\citet{guo2017calibration}.

\subsection{Runtime Acceptance Gate}
\label{sec:gate}

Not every classifier output is forwarded to the report writer. The
runtime applies a per-class confidence threshold:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{accept}(x)
= \mathbf{1}\!\left[\bar{p}(\hat{y}(x)\mid x) \ge \tau_{\hat{y}(x)}\right].
\label{eq:threshold-gate}
\end{equation}
The deployed thresholds are
\begin{align}
\tau_{\mathrm{healthy}} &= 0.40, &
\tau_{\mathrm{overwatered}} &= 0.60, &
\tau_{\mathrm{root\ rot}} &= 0.65, \nonumber \\
\tau_{\mathrm{drought}} &= 0.65, &
\tau_{\mathrm{frost}} &= 0.70, &
\tau_{\mathrm{gray\ mold}} &= 0.60, \quad
\tau_{\mathrm{white\ mold}} = 0.60.
\label{eq:class-thresholds}
\end{align}
The default for any unmapped class is $0.55$. A second guard demotes the
prediction to the explicit \texttt{unknown} state when the accepted
top-1 probability drops below $0.50$ after prompt assembly. The
runtime additionally records the top-1 and top-2 probabilities $p_1, p_2$
and the margin $\Delta = p_1 - p_2$ to a structured decision log; a
margin threshold of $0.08$ is currently logged but not enforced. The
classifier accuracy figures reported in \S\ref{sec:results} are
threshold-only and do not depend on margin gating.

\subsection{Detector-Grounded Prompt Assembly}

Downstream of the classifier, the runtime invokes RF-DETR
Small~\citep{robinson2025rfdetr,carion2020detr} to produce a
multi-label visibility vector over the six-part vocabulary
$\mathcal{P} = \{\mathrm{flower}, \mathrm{fruit}, \mathrm{leaf},
\mathrm{root}, \mathrm{soil}, \mathrm{stem}\}$. Per-part thresholds are
tuned to each class:
\begin{align*}
\theta_{\mathrm{flower}} &= 0.50, &
\theta_{\mathrm{fruit}} &= 0.50, &
\theta_{\mathrm{leaf}} &= 0.40, \\
\theta_{\mathrm{root}} &= 0.30, &
\theta_{\mathrm{soil}} &= 0.40, &
\theta_{\mathrm{stem}} &= 0.40.
\end{align*}
Let $V(x) \subseteq \mathcal{P}$ denote the set of parts whose maximum
detection confidence exceeds the corresponding threshold. The prompt
assembler constructs visible-part and not-visible-part lists,
$V(x)$ and $\mathcal{P}\setminus V(x)$, and instructs the language model
to describe symptoms only on parts in $V(x)$. A small post-processing
heuristic remaps mentions of \emph{leaf} in mold-disease contexts to
\emph{fruit} when the surrounding sentence references the calyx, since
the green sepals of a strawberry fruit are commonly miscategorized as
foliage in free-text descriptions.

\subsection{Optional Retrieval and Hallucination Retry}

The runtime stack includes two additional support modules:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.3em]
    \item \textbf{Knowledge-graph retrieval.} A local disease knowledge
    base, populated from cooperative-extension sources, exposes a
    retrieval interface following the pattern of
    \citet{lewis2020rag}. When the accepted label is not
    \texttt{unknown}, retrieved snippets are appended to the
    report-writer prompt as supplemental context. Treatment passages may
    be conditioned on detected parts (for example, fruit-specific advice
    when \emph{fruit}~$\in V(x)$).
    \item \textbf{Heuristic hallucination retry.} A phrase-pattern matcher
    inspects the generated report for a curated list of overclaim
    triggers (for instance, references to disease names that disagree
    with the classifier label, or claims about parts in
    $\mathcal{P}\setminus V(x)$). When a trigger fires, the runtime
    re-issues the generation. This module is explicitly heuristic and
    has not yet been calibrated against a false-positive budget.
\end{enumerate}
All three modules execute locally; no external model is queried at
inference time.

\subsection{Report Writer Configuration}

The report-writer backbone is MiniGPT-v2~\citep{chen2023minigptv2}, with
a Llama 2 chat backbone~\citep{touvron2023llama2} and LoRA
adaptation~\citep{hu2021lora}. The active training configuration uses
448-pixel image inputs, a maximum text length of 500 tokens, LoRA rank
$r=64$ with $\alpha=128$ and dropout $0.05$, batch size 2 per GPU with 8
gradient-accumulation steps, and 20 epochs with a 10\% auto-validation
split. These are the active repository settings; we note in
\S\ref{sec:results} that the deployed checkpoint predates the constrained
stage-2 redesign.

\subsection{Constrained Dual-Report Supervision Policy}
\label{sec:dualpolicy}

Stage-2 supervision targets are generated offline from a frontier model
(used only at dataset-construction time). For each input image, the
policy emits a primary farmer-facing report. A second report is emitted
only if the classifier exposes a \emph{semantically plausible} close
alternative, where plausibility is determined by both a margin condition
and a curated pair whitelist.

Let $g(x)$ denote the ground-truth folder label, $y_1(x)$ the top-1
classifier label, and $y_2(x)$ the top-2 classifier label. The competing
class used by the policy is
\begin{equation}
a(x) =
\begin{cases}
y_2(x), & y_1(x) = g(x), \\
y_1(x), & y_1(x) \neq g(x).
\end{cases}
\label{eq:alt-class}
\end{equation}
The allowed pair set is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{A} =
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
&\{\mathrm{gray\ mold}, \mathrm{white\ mold}\}, \\
&\{\mathrm{drought}, \mathrm{overwatered}\}, \\
&\{\mathrm{drought}, \mathrm{frost}\}, \\
&\{\mathrm{healthy}, \mathrm{overwatered}\}, \\
&\{\mathrm{root\ rot}, \mathrm{overwatered}\}.
\end{aligned}
\right\}
\label{eq:allowed-pairs}
\end{equation}
A second report is labelled \emph{ambiguous} only when one of two
conditions holds:
\begin{align}
y_1(x) = g(x), \qquad
\bar{p}(y_1 \mid x) - \bar{p}(y_2 \mid x) &\le 0.10, \nonumber \\
\bar{p}(y_2 \mid x) \ge 0.15, \qquad
\{g(x), y_2(x)\} &\in \mathcal{A}, \label{eq:amb-case-1} \\
y_1(x) \neq g(x), \qquad
\mathrm{rank}(g(x)) = 2, \nonumber \\
\bar{p}(y_1 \mid x) - \bar{p}(g(x) \mid x) &\le 0.10, \nonumber \\
\bar{p}(g(x) \mid x) \ge 0.15, \qquad
\{g(x), y_1(x)\} &\in \mathcal{A}. \label{eq:amb-case-2}
\end{align}
When the classifier is correct and the margin is wide, the second report
is emitted as a \emph{standard variant}: a paraphrase under the same
diagnostic label, used to expand training-target diversity. When the
classifier is incorrect and the ground-truth label is not a close
rank-2 competitor, no second report is emitted, which prevents
classifier failures from being relabelled as ambiguity events. The
policy is restricted to dataset construction and does not currently
imply any user-facing alternative-presentation behavior at runtime.
